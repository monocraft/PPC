# Viewing products and exporting slides

Collapsed products have **Specifications** and **Dates** icons aligned at the right edge above their SKU footer. Hover an icon to see its label. Specifications opens the complete technical details alongside the original product image and name. Dates opens the six milestones and their history directly. Once the reading area opens, its top tabs provide navigation and the duplicate icon pair disappears from the selected card. The Details tabs also retain HP SKUs and More. Use the close control in the information area to return to the overview and its icons. Keyboard focus moves to the requested reading tab, then returns to the opener when closing from the reading area. Icon placement stays consistent across different color-SKU counts and the temporary reading extension.

Each category remembers its product zoom and roadmap zoom on this device, including after a reload. These small view preferences are separate from the portfolio backup and shared data. Fit and the zoom buttons update the current category's preference. A category with no remembered preference uses the existing readable automatic Fit.

Hovering between products uses a quiet moving guide. Scrolling the roadmap keeps its calendar header fixed. The product editor keeps its title, actions and five section tabs together while its content scrolls.

Lane and card order changes made through Reorder products or the editor are shared product edits. They appear in Save to master review and remain in the accepted shared placement after refresh. Lane and order are reviewed together; a concurrent change to an affected lane requires an explicit choice. Device saving keeps a draft until it is shared.

After an accepted team save, a delayed downloadable copy is reported separately. Dismissing **Downloadable copy delayed** acknowledges that publication issue across retries and reloads; its reason and Check status remain in Messages. A confirmed successful publication clears the acknowledgment. Failed team saves remain visible while edits are pending.

PowerPoint export offers separate category roadmaps or one combined roadmap, with an explicit start and end month. The dialog reports products outside the selected period; products whose dates remain to be confirmed stay included. Names, lifecycle bars and status information remain editable, and the saved MSRP visibility option is respected.

The combined roadmap uses 7 pt product names, 0.08-inch lifecycle bars and 0.16-inch product rows. It shows each product individually. A three-track calendar separates years, quarters and months using the site's hierarchy, graphite tones and restrained separators. Category coverage summaries are removed.

Merged slides use exactly one shared calendar and one ordered stack of individual product timelines. Small selections use the standard widescreen canvas. Larger selections use a larger 16:9 canvas within PowerPoint's size limit. Category and family labels preserve context without repeated calendars.

The export dialog measures the selected period, full names, optional prices, unconfirmed dates and grouping labels before making a file. If the selection exceeds one slide, it shows how many products fit, the last included product and the first product beyond the cutoff. Export remains disabled until the user reduces the selection or explicitly chooses **Export only the products that fit**. That choice includes only the displayed ordered prefix, with the excluded count clearly reported. Changing the categories, period or export scope, or reopening the dialog, clears that choice so another selection cannot inherit permission to omit products. A calendar that exceeds the slide limit requires a shorter period.

PowerPoint uses one canvas size for an entire presentation. When Both includes a larger merged roadmap, the product portfolio slides scale proportionally to that canvas. Product portfolio continuation pages still start with the first occupied lane at the top; sparse pages retain the same product scale as fuller pages.

Interactive card actions and temporary reading areas are omitted from PNG and PowerPoint exports. Product images, complete specifications, dates, SKUs and source records are retained.

All PowerPoint exports use **HP Forma DJR Office**, including titles, product cards, roadmap labels, calendars, prices, legends, masters, notes and theme defaults. The supplied regular, medium, bold and italic styles are permitted; other font families are not permitted in exported files. Export measures and renders with this font, then restores the website's Jost font. Local Office font faces load from installed fonts for measurement; the supplied TTF files are not published as website assets. If a browser's font check reports the font missing, Export asks for the supplied font to be installed instead of creating a file with fallback measurements. PowerPoint recipients need the HP Office font installed to display its exact glyphs.

## Runtime validation

The focused view-runtime checks exercise the actual browser functions against controlled fixtures: event coalescing, per-category zoom replay, data-revision cache invalidation, retained Details DOM, hidden-editor avoidance, stable canvas allocation, viewport-only screen painting, full export coverage and unchanged native scroll offsets. The export checks exercise the actual application planner and native editable slide renderer, including dense-layout decisions, period filtering, unscheduled products, prices and family order.

Measured browser comparisons use a separate, identically populated local preview. They are controlled task-duration comparisons, not a claim about frame rate or every device. Private receipts remain outside the published site.
